**Technique pointer.** This is MASTG's "reviewing decompiled code" technique (MASVS-STORAGE-1). Open **MASDojo.apk** (the target you downloaded) in **jadx-gui** (or run `jadx -d out MASDojo.apk`) and browse the app's own package, `org.masdojo.vaultbank`.
