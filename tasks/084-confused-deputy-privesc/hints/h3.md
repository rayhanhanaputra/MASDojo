**Concrete steps.**
1. Confirm reachability: the receiver is `exported="true"` with no permission, so a low-privileged caller can trigger it, e.g. `adb shell am broadcast -a org.masdojo.vaultbank.action.ELEVATE --es requester attacker org.masdojo.vaultbank`.
2. The grant it returns isn't cleartext — `decodeGrant()` hex-decodes `ENC` and XORs each byte with `GRANT_KEY`. Replay that on *your* seeded values to reconstruct the grant `FLAG{...}` and submit it.
