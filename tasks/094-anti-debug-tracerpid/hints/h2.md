**Technique pointer.** Anti-debug bypass (MASVS-RESILIENCE-4, MASTG-TECH-0043). The check reads `TracerPid` from `/proc/self/status`; a non-zero value means someone is ptrace-tracing the process. You can either patch what the reader sees, or — far simpler — hook the one boolean method that interprets it. The class/method are in the artifact.
